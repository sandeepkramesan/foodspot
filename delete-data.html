Your FoodSpot account and spots

Your account holds your email address (and Google ID and name if you log in with Google), your username, your saved spots and notes (public and private), who you follow, and alert settings.

  • In the app: Profile → your initial (top right) → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, username, every saved spot, your follows and followers, and any creator profile you claimed.
  • Just some spots: delete them in the app (long-press to select several). The deletion syncs to your account and your other phones.
  • By email: write to privacy@foodspot.app from the address you log in with. We delete the account within 30 days.

Logging out or uninstalling FoodSpot only removes the copy on that phone; your account stays until you delete it.

If you claimed a creator profile

We store the IDs, handles and names of the accounts you connected (no passwords or access tokens). To delete them:

  • In the app: open your profile → “You manage this profile · Options” → Remove profile. This deletes your claim and every connected account immediately.
  • From Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → remove FoodSpot. Instagram tells us, and we delete your Instagram connection and the profile it claimed automatically.
  • From Google: remove FoodSpot at myaccount.google.com/permissions, then remove the YouTube connection in the app (or email us).
  • By email: write to privacy@foodspot.app from an address we can verify, with your profile handle. We delete it within 30 days.
