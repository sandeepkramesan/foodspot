03 About FoodSpot

What FoodSpot does.

FoodSpot is a free Android app, made by ftwco., for saving the restaurants you discover in Instagram and YouTube food videos. Share a reel to FoodSpot and it reads the caption, finds the restaurant, and pins it on your personal food map. Later, tap the pin for directions in Google Maps.

Pick a username to get your own profile. Keep each spot public or private, follow friends and creators, rate places, and respot their finds into your own list.

Your FoodSpot account

You log in so your saved spots are kept in your account and stay with you when you change phones. You can log in with a one-time code sent to your email, or with Sign in with Google.

When you sign in with Google, FoodSpot receives only your name, email address and Google account ID. We use them only to create your FoodSpot account, log you in and sync your saved spots. We don’t use them for ads, don’t sell them and don’t share them. You can delete your account and all its data in the app at any time. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

05 Questions

Good to know.

Something else? Write to support@foodspot.app.

Is FoodSpot free?

Yes, completely: no ads and no subscriptions.

Is it on the Play Store or iPhone?

Not yet. FoodSpot is Android-only for now and installs from this page. Play Store and iPhone may come later.

Which reels work?

Any Instagram reel or YouTube Short (and most web pages or Google Maps links). FoodSpot works best when the caption names the place, like “📍 Restaurant, Area, City”. If it can’t tell, it shows its best guess and you pick the spot on the map.

Who can see my spots?

You choose for each spot. Public spots show on your profile and on the maps of people who follow you; private spots are only yours. Spots you saved before profiles existed stay private.

Where is my data stored?

In your FoodSpot account, so your spots follow you to a new phone, with a copy on your phone for offline use. You can delete your account in the app at any time. Read the privacy policy.

I’m a creator. Can I link my profile?

Soon: you’ll be able to connect your Instagram or YouTube to your FoodSpot profile, so fans can follow the places from your reels.
